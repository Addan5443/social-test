Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Social Test is a private development application used to test social media account authorization and content publishing workflows.

Information We Process

When an authorized test user connects a social media account, the application may process basic account information made available through the platform's authorized API, authentication tokens required to maintain the authorized connection, and video content and captions selected by the authorized user for testing.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized test accounts, test API integrations, upload or publish content requested by the authorized user, and troubleshoot the development workflow.

Sharing

Social Test does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to the relevant social media platform when necessary to perform an authorized test action.

Data Retention and Security

Test data is retained only as needed for development and testing. Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and authentication information from unauthorized access.

User Control

Authorized users may revoke the application's access through the applicable social media platform's account settings where supported.

Scope

This application is intended for private development and testing and is not currently offered as a public consumer service.
